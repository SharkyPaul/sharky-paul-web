About

Underwater since 2010.

My first dives were in the summer of 2010 on the island of Brač, Croatia – wrecks at 28 metres, a cave and my first night dive. Since then, every holiday I have has ended up in the ocean.

The moment that changed everything was in Marsa Alam, Egypt: my first shark, an oceanic whitetip. Not a monster – a calm, curious animal in its own home. That encounter became Sharky Paul.

Today I create short videos that replace shark myths with verified facts and give holidaymakers practical safety advice for the Red Sea. I write free guides, speak to guests and produce my own music – Afro Tribal Bounce.

The ocean is not a water park. It is their world – we are only guests there.— SHARKY PAUL
PADI Advanced Open Water Diver SDI Open Water Diver Tactical first-aid course (THOR Tactical) Author of 2 ocean guides
